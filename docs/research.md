## Opportunity assessment

Strengths: distinctive Medilatino identity, published menus and offers, multiple booking/ordering touchpoints, a designed social space, and positive visible Google feedback. The restaurant's designer documents an open kitchen and green terrace. Likely visitor occasions include dates, friends sharing a meal, and local/expat social gatherings; this is an audience inference.

Three weaknesses observed:
1. The existing website presents a hero and generic contact form; no menu navigation, hours/address, or clear restaurant-specific booking journey appeared in the inspected page.
2. Practical information is scattered across Linktree and dining platforms, with inconsistent directory hours.
3. The website undersells the current taco/kebab identity and distinctive social setting.

Three valuable improvements:
1. Scannable, filterable real dish highlights and an obvious current-menu action.
2. A mobile visit journey leading to the restaurant's real booking channel.
3. Cross-verified directions, hours, phone/email and social links in one location.

Owner value proposition: demonstrate a cohesive brand and lower browsing/contact friction without promising business outcomes. Business specificity comes from the Medilatino concept, actual menu/price facts, Soi Si Bamphen location, open-kitchen/terrace story and official destination links. No competitor was cloned; the existing business sources were sufficient.

## Architecture and asset policy

Single static page: introduction → food → atmosphere → feedback → visit planner → location/contact → FAQs/disclosures. The conceptual artwork and food imagery are labeled; no authentic photos were available with established reuse permission. Food imagery generated with the built-in image tool is stored locally in assets/sharing-table.webp. The hero's prompt and full provenance are in README.md.

No fabricated awards, credentials, reviews, availability or transaction confirmations. No paid services. Public demo is explicitly unaffiliated and requests no search indexing.
